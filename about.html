About Me

I'm Tucker Yazdani, a Data Engineer based in Chicago with three years of experience designing scalable ETL pipelines and modern data infrastructure. My expertise lies in automating complex workflows across cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, Snowflake, and Databricks to support enterprise analytics and reporting.

I specialize in building end-to-end data solutions using Python, SQL, and Airflow—from integrating APIs and orchestrating secure data transfers to developing MDM pipelines and deploying business intelligence dashboards with Tableau and Power BI.

Outside of my professional roles, I maintain The Reddit Street Journal, an automated data pipeline that scrapes Reddit for trending stock mentions and publishes insights via Twitter. This website also serves as a living portfolio of my ongoing projects and technical interests.

I hold a B.S. in Mathematics with a concentration in Statistics and Actuarial Science from the University of West Georgia. I'm driven by a passion for simplifying complex data challenges and building tools that deliver measurable value.
