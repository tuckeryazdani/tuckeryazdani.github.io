01 · Data pipeline & publishing automation

Reddit Street Journal

A personal data product that turns fast-moving market conversations into structured weekly summaries.

Completed
June 2024

01

Problem

Stock conversations on Reddit are noisy and change quickly, making it difficult to identify frequently discussed tickers and add useful market context consistently.

02

My contribution

Designed and built the automated workflow that collects discussions, detects stock mentions, enriches results with Yahoo Finance data, generates summaries, and publishes the output.

03

Technology

  • Python
  • SQL
  • Docker
  • GCP
  • Yahoo Finance
  • GitHub Pages
04

Outcome

Created a repeatable publishing workflow that converts unstructured social discussion into structured, timely market summaries.
